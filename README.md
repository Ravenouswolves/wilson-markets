# Wilson Markets

A phone-first stocks & finance tracker in a single `index.html`: a morning brief, a heat map of your holdings, your portfolio, a watchlist with a quality score and insider trades, price alerts, and short lessons.

- Opens with **sample data**. To see live prices and news, add your own free [Finnhub](https://finnhub.io/) API key in Settings and switch to Live. An optional free [Twelve Data](https://twelvedata.com/) key turns on price charts.
- Everything (holdings, alerts, keys) stays in your browser's localStorage. You never sign in to a brokerage.
- Educational only, not financial advice.
